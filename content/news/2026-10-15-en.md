    summary: "In the terminal, a conversation over 200K tokens left idle for about 54 minutes is compacted before its one-hour prompt cache expires, so your next message doesn't reprocess the whole thing. CLAUDE_CODE_IDLE_COMPACT_MIN_TOKENS changes the 200K minimum, and \"idleCompaction\": false in settings.json turns it off. Both need v2.1.290 or later. Whether it also runs outside the terminal (VS Code, desktop app) is not disclosed."
    comment: "Long conversations getting compacted is natural. What matters is whether it happens when you want it to. I always compact manually. The right moments are when one task is done, when one category of a long task is done, or before I step away for a long time. A cache miss with a nearly full context is a waste."

    points:
      - "The OpenTelemetry api_request event adds cache_creation_5m_tokens and cache_creation_1h_tokens, splitting cache writes by lifetime."
      - "Trace spans get OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic-convention attributes such as gen_ai.provider.name, gen_ai.conversation.id, gen_ai.tool.name and error.type."
      - "Fixed resuming a headless or Agent SDK session losing the recent messages a compaction kept, when the compaction followed a reply cut at the output token limit. Applies to compactions from this version on."
      - "Fixed resuming losing the compaction summary or bringing back pre-compaction history, when the compaction followed a retried or unfinished reply in the same turn."
      - "Fixed the first transcript entry written after resuming being lost when a crash had cut the transcript's last line short."
