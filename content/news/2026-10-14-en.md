**The conclusion first:** On the early morning of October 14 (KST), Google released its new top model, Gemini 4 Argon, worldwide. On Google's own table it leads 12 of 18 benchmarks, but on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard (2026-10-14) Argon (high) ties GPT-6 Astra (max) at 53 and sits below Claude Opus 5.5 (max) at 58. The differences that stand out are the hallucination rate (15%) and output that can run to 1M tokens in one response.

## When it shipped, and what shipped

- **Announcement:** October 1, 2026, 05:00 KST. At first it was offered only to defensive security organizations through the Fairwind program. ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/))
- **General release:** October 14, 2026, 01:00 KST. It rolled out to paid Gemini API tiers, AI Studio, Google Antigravity (Ultra subscribers), and the Gemini app and Search AI Mode (Pro and Ultra subscribers). ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/))
- **Model ID:** `gemini-4-argon`. The default thinking level is medium, and low, medium, or high can be selected. ([Gemini API docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/latest-model))

## Main capabilities

- **Input, thinking, and output share one 1,048,576-token window.** A single response can pass 64K and run up to 1M tokens (Long decode). ([Gemini API docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/latest-model))
- **In Antigravity it can be selected as the model for running `/goal` and for coordinating multiple agents.** ([AI Studio developer guide](https://aistudio.google.com/learn/gemini-4-argon-developer-guide)) How `/goal` judges inside a harness is written up in [Claude Code vs Codex /goal](/posts/agentic/claude-code-codex-goal).
- **Tuned for defensive security.** It was trained to find, verify, and patch vulnerabilities, and trusted defensive organizations get it without the cyber guardrails. ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/))
- **Google's internal examples (Google's announcement):** a C/C++ to Rust migration, a libgav1 decoder 2.7x faster than the previous Rust port, and more than 300 TiB of data-center memory saved.

## Community reaction

- **Hacker News:** The launch thread reached 1,579 points and 1,048 comments by October 1. The most common complaint was "nobody can use it yet," followed by doubts about the benchmarks. ([endue briefing](https://endue.ai/blog/gemini-4-argon-briefing/), [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49914236))
- **Split takes on HN:** One view was that intelligence and cost per task are almost the same as Opus 5.5 High. Another was that it is disappointing. ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49914236))
- **Reddit r/singularity:** The 15% hallucination rate drew the most interest. At the same time, people pointed out that the cost advantage Artificial Analysis cited uses the introductory price, and that the regular price is higher than Astra. ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/singularity/comments/1wug9tb/google_gemini_4_scores_same_as_gpt_6_astra_on/))
- **Skepticism inside Google:** Bloomberg reported that some employees doubt the real coding performance, and Google disputed that report. ([AT Pulse](https://at-pulse.com/stories/1987/))
